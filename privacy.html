Deep Sea Diver — Privacy Policy

Effective: August 17, 2026
Deep Sea Diver does not collect any personal information.

Information We Collect

None. Deep Sea Diver:

  • Does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.
  • Is a fully offline game with no network communication whatsoever.
  • Contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking tools.
  • Requires no account or sign-in.

Data Stored on Your Device

Game progress (cleared levels, observation log, pearls and gear upgrades) is stored only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data.
